# Chapter 2. Research questions, data and evaluation

The project asks whether the observations an LMS already records can help a teacher identify assessment cases worth reviewing at an acceptable false-positive rate. For quiz behaviour the unit of analysis is a submitted attempt. For text reuse it is a pair comprising a submitted document and a potential source. For teacher review it is a person's decision on a case. Each unit needs its own comparison and denominator. [Verification plan](../08_verification/verification_plan.md)

## Behavioural data and experimental design

The initial quiz generator creates students with different response speeds and revision habits. It produces ordinary attempts as well as attempts with injected patterns such as very short response times or synchronized submissions. This makes it possible to check that a model can recover a deliberately inserted pattern. It also favours detectors that use the same variables as the generator. An alert on a student whose answer times were shortened by construction offers limited evidence about a student who obtained the questions in advance. [Dataset protocol](../../ml/notebooks/datasets.md) · [Experiment report](../06_reports/article-experiment-2026-11.md)

The second experiment changes how anomalous behaviour arises. The simulator reuses learner and item characteristics across related worlds. In one world it records honest attempts. In another it injects anomalies along feature dimensions. In a third, students act according to specified mechanisms, including answer lookup, copying after a delay, advance knowledge and proxy test-taking. The scoring pipeline receives the resulting events. Mechanism labels stay outside the feature matrix. Paired worlds let the experiment compare detectors under similar student populations even when the actions behind the labels differ. [Simulator experiment](../06_reports/article-experiment-2026-11.md)

EdNet supplies observed learner response behaviour for calibration. The calibration report records 146,638 learners, of whom 117,392 went into the fitting partition and 29,246 into the holdout partition. Repeat exposures and the source of a learning activity matter when interpreting rapid responses. The calibrated timing model still represents assumptions about an exam because EdNet records self-study. Its events carry no adjudicated cheating labels. [EdNet sample and exclusions](../../ml/ednet/calibration_report.md) · [EdNet transfer evaluation](../../ml/experiments/inflation/results/tables/ednet.md)

## H1. Behavioural detection

H1 predicts that adding multivariate and peer information improves recall at a fixed false-positive operating point. The primary contrast is Isolation Forest with peers against training-quantile rules. A second forest omits the peer features, allowing the experiment to examine their contribution. The rules combine extreme values of individual features. The forest assigns an anomaly score according to how readily its random splits isolate an attempt [@liu2008isolation]. The rules comparator also receives peer features. A gain in this contrast would therefore concern the scoring method on the available evidence. [Primary comparison](../../ml/experiments/inflation/results/tables/h1.md) · [Detector definitions](../../ml/experiments/inflation/README.md)

For each attempt, first responses determine the timing summaries. Revisions retain their order, and comparable earlier attempts supply the student's score history. A peer must take the same quiz and variant in the same cohort. Final answers can then be aligned by question and compared within a submission-time window. Training data supply the item-time reference. These choices account for systematic variation across students and questions [@vanderlinden2006lognormal] and distinguish agreement on particular items from the simple fact that two people finished together [@wollack1997copying]. [Feature and split protocol](../../ml/notebooks/baseline_plan.md)

The main experiment separates whole cohorts into training, validation and test partitions in proportions of 60%, 20% and 20%. The primary fit uses ordinary training attempts. Validation sets the alert threshold under a 5% FPR constraint, and the held-out cohorts measure recall and the realized FPR at that threshold. Precision is the share of alerts on labelled anomalous attempts. Recall is the share of labelled anomalous attempts flagged. FPR uses the number of ordinary attempts as its denominator. The primary hypothesis requires a recall gain of at least 15 percentage points in both prespecified comparisons. Mean gains below 5 points in both lead to adoption of the rules. [Experimental protocol](../../ml/experiments/inflation/README.md) · [Decision criteria](../../ml/experiments/inflation/results/tables/h1.md)

Across 20 simulator replicates, the peer-enabled forest detected 61.0% of the peer-copying attempts and the rules detected 65.5%. The paired difference was −4.5 percentage points, with a 95% interval from −7.4 to −1.2. For proxy test-taking the corresponding recalls were 9.5% and 10.7%, a difference of −1.2 points with an interval from −3.8 to 1.4. The second interval includes zero. Both means fell below the adoption boundary, so the project selected the rules under this protocol. [H1 results by mechanism](../../ml/experiments/inflation/results/tables/h1.md) · [Replicate design](../06_reports/article-experiment-2026-11.md)

The fitting assumption matters in practice. If contaminated attempts enter the reference sample, extreme observations can move the quantiles used for screening. Under the contamination experiment the relative advantage of the methods changed. Calibration also matters when moving from simulation to live data. At a nominal 5% operating point, models fitted on synthetic ordinary behaviour flagged roughly 11–39% of real EdNet attempts. These are observed alert rates. Estimating FPR would require knowing which attempts were ordinary. The planned evaluation on anonymized course data remains open. [Contamination experiment](../../ml/experiments/inflation/results/tables/protocol.md) · [EdNet results](../../ml/experiments/inflation/results/tables/ednet.md) · [Experiment report](../06_reports/article-experiment-2026-11.md)

## H2. Teacher review

H2 concerns the time needed to reach a decision and the teacher's agreement with an alert. The planned pilot compares review of raw attempt data with review of a case that also supplies an explanation and supporting observations. It calls for at least 2 teachers and at least 20 cases per teacher. The time target is a 30% reduction in median review time. The second outcome is the proportion of reviewed decisions that confirm the alert, with a confidence interval. That quantity measures agreement with the signal. Estimating agreement between teachers would require their independent ratings on shared cases. [Pilot design and current status](../08_verification/verification_plan.md)

The pilot has no reported teacher times or decisions yet. A platform replay can verify that an explanation reaches the teacher's case record, but the pilot must measure how a person uses it. Missing reviews stay distinguishable from dismissals. An audit sample of unflagged attempts is also necessary to estimate how often the system missed cases that merited review. [Review protocol](../08_verification/verification_plan.md) · [Behavioural evidence review](../05_literature/anomaly_detection.md)

## H3. Retrieval of reused text

The public PAN-PC-11 corpus supplies source documents, suspicious documents and annotated pairs with character spans. The experiment groups every source with all documents connected to it by annotated reuse. An entire connected family stays in one partition. Without that constraint, a copy used to select a threshold could share a source with a copy in the test set. A qualifying H3 pair contains at least one literal or lightly edited passage of 50 or more normalized tokens on the target side, with valid annotation on both sides. Other known positives stay outside the H3 comparison. All test pairs without an annotated source link serve as negatives. [PAN protocol and denominators](../../ml/integrity/results.md)

The lexical baseline computes five-token shingles over 200-token windows at a stride of 100. A 128-permutation MinHash signature and locality-sensitive hashing select candidate windows [@broder1997resemblance] [@gionis1999similarity]. Exact Jaccard then verifies each candidate and the system recovers matching spans at original character offsets. Exhaustive Jaccard examines the same reference bank without the approximate retrieval step. A TF-IDF cosine baseline fits its vocabulary on training documents [@salton1988term]. Validation selects the Jaccard cutoff and minimum evidence length before scoring the test set. The three quality targets are pair precision of at least 0.90, pair recall of at least 0.85 and raw candidate recall of at least 0.95. [Baseline specification](../../ml/integrity/README.md) · [Test criteria and measured settings](../../ml/integrity/results.md)

The initial test used 120 documents. The larger experiment used 360 documents per run at seeds 42 through 46. The test splits contained five to eight positive families overall, but only one to four pairs met the H3 passage definition per split. Candidate hits, in seed order, were 1/4, 0/4, 1/3, 0/2 and 0/1. At the validation-selected Jaccard cutoff of 0.80 and minimum evidence length of 50 tokens, none survived verification. Pair recall was zero in all five runs. Precision was undefined because no pairs were flagged in the H3 comparison. The exhaustive comparator at the same verification settings also returned no qualifying pair, so approximate retrieval alone cannot account for the final misses. [Counts, denominators and frozen comparisons](../../ml/integrity/results.md)

At seed 42, whole-document signatures retrieved none of four qualifying pairs. With windows, increasing the number of permutations from 128 to 256 increased candidate hits from one to two, while final pair recall stayed at zero. On the five larger baseline runs, LSH retrieval and exact window checks took between 2.78% and 6.95% of the comparable exhaustive retrieval time. This comparison starts after shared indexing and query preparation. The faster search missed the accuracy targets. [Ablations and retrieval timings](../../ml/integrity/results.md)

Uncertainty is especially large because few qualifying families reach test. The report resamples whole query families against a fixed reference bank. Connected families can recur across seeds, so adding the hits across runs and treating them as independent observations would overstate the amount of evidence. Three oversized connected families were excluded under the selection rule. The conclusion applies to these closed subsets with their observed mix of reuse, and an annotated source link measures textual reuse. Deciding whether a student breached an assignment rule requires further evidence about attribution and permitted collaboration.
