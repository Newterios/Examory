# Structured analysis of publications

This table follows the entry order in [sources.bib](sources.bib). Findings, project uses and caveats summarize the [plagiarism review](plagiarism_detection.md) and [anomaly and proctoring review](anomaly_detection.md). Project uses describe the choices recorded in those reviews, including deferred experiments, rather than measured implementation outcomes. Types follow the BibTeX entry and venue: workshop and Findings proceedings are classified as conference papers; arXiv preprints remain preprints. Counts use the BibTeX `year` field, including documentation and legal acts.

| 1 | `broder1997resemblance` | Andrei Z. Broder, 1997 | conference paper | Shingling, MinHash, resemblance and containment | Under a random permutation, MinHash collision probability equals Jaccard similarity; directional containment distinguishes a copied passage from whole-document resemblance. | integrity: ground the text baseline in shingles and MinHash, with chunk matching and exact overlap evidence. | Low whole-document Jaccard can hide partial borrowing; substantial paraphrasing destroys lexical shingles. |
| 2 | `gionis1999similarity` | Aristides Gionis; Piotr Indyk; Rajeev Motwani, 1999 | conference paper | Locality-sensitive hashing | LSH retrieves likely similar candidates without comparing every pair. | integrity: retrieve MinHash candidates, verify them with actual shingles, and measure candidate recall against exact search. | Approximate retrieval can miss candidates; banding gives a probability curve rather than a guaranteed similarity threshold. |
| 3 | `salton1988term` | Gerard Salton; Christopher Buckley, 1988 | journal article | TF-IDF and cosine similarity | Term weighting reduces common-term influence; sparse vectors provide an inexpensive lexical similarity baseline. | integrity: compare MinHash against TF-IDF cosine, fitting vocabulary and IDF only on training/reference data. | Shared assignment vocabulary can dominate; synonyms and translation remain difficult, and test-based fitting or threshold selection leaks information. |
| 4 | `reimers2019sentence` | Nils Reimers; Iryna Gurevych, 2019 | conference paper | Sentence-BERT and Sentence-RoBERTa | Siamese/triplet training produces cosine-comparable embeddings that can be cached and retrieve paraphrases missed by shingles. | integrity: evaluate passage embeddings as a later semantic-retrieval ablation, separately by language and task. | Semantic similarity does not establish copying; correct short answers may share meaning, and language transfer requires evaluation. |
